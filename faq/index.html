Frequently asked questions

General questions about booking with Naqaa. Each service page also has its own questions.

Booking and prices

How do I book?

Send the home size, the service and your area on WhatsApp, or use the quick quote. We reply with the price and the available times, and the booking is confirmed when you agree.

Is the price fixed?

The price we agree on WhatsApp is the price you pay for the work described. If you add extra work on the day, the team will tell you the cost before doing it.

Do you bring materials and equipment?

Yes, for every service.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Message us as early as you can and we'll move the visit to another available time.

Do I need to be at home?

It's best to be there at the start and for the final walkthrough. You can arrange access with building security in between.

Do you offer pest control?

Not in the UAE at the moment. Our services are cleaning only.

Questions by service

Home cleaning

Do I need to provide cleaning materials or a vacuum?

No. The team brings its own equipment and materials. If you want a specific product used on a surface, such as marble or wood, tell us and we'll use yours.

Will the same cleaner come every time?

For regular bookings we try to keep the same team so they learn your home and your preferences. If someone is off, we tell you before the visit.

Deep cleaning

How long does a deep clean take?

It depends on the size and condition of the home. When you send the details we tell you how many people we'll send and roughly how long they'll need.

Do you clean inside the oven and fridge?

Yes, on request. Please empty the fridge before the team arrives.

Move-in / move-out cleaning

Does the unit need to be empty?

It's best if it is. If some furniture stays, tell us which rooms are affected so we plan the time correctly.

Can you clean on the same day as the movers?

Yes, if the movers finish first. Tell us their expected finish time and we'll schedule the team after it.

Sofa & carpet cleaning

Will every stain come out?

Many stains lift fully, but old stains, ink, dye transfer and bleach marks may only fade. The team will tell you before starting which stains are unlikely to come out.

How long until I can use the sofa?

It depends on the fabric and the room's airflow. Keep the AC or a fan on and wait until it feels dry to the touch.

Water tank cleaning

How long will we be without water?

Only while the tank is drained, cleaned and refilled. We tell you the expected time when we confirm the booking, and we can work early in the day to keep the interruption short.

How often should a tank be cleaned?

Many owners clean at least once a year, and more often if the water looks cloudy or the tank is exposed to dust. Follow any schedule your building management or local authority sets.

Office cleaning

Do you clean after office hours?

Yes. Evening, early-morning and weekend visits are available. We agree on access and alarm arrangements before the first visit.

Who provides the washroom supplies?

Cleaning materials and equipment are ours. Consumables such as soap, tissue and bin liners are usually supplied by the office, or we can agree to supply them.

Ready for a cleaner place this week?

Send the details on WhatsApp and get your price before anything is booked.
