Cleaning towers, townhouses and villas

Most Dubai homes are high-rise apartments, where the team works with building security, service lifts and set working hours. In villa and townhouse communities the job is bigger: more bathrooms, terraces, maid's rooms and often a water tank on the roof. We plan the team size and time around the type of home you send us.

Building access in Dubai

Many towers and communities register service teams at the gate or ask for an access request from the resident. Tell us your building and the hours it allows, and register the team if needed, so the visit starts on time. For move-out cleaning, check whether your building needs a move permit on the same day.
