When a deep clean makes sense

Book a deep clean when the home needs a reset rather than upkeep: after the summer, when you return from a long trip, before a new baby arrives, after renovation dust, or when you take over a home and want to start clean. It's also the right first visit before switching to regular home cleaning.

What makes it different from regular cleaning

A regular clean keeps visible surfaces tidy. A deep clean goes into the build-up: grease that has hardened on the hood and the tiles behind the hob, hard-water marks on the shower glass, the dust line along the top of cabinets and wardrobes, and the black edges of grout and silicone in humid bathrooms. It takes longer and the team uses different products and tools for each surface.

Humidity, mould and AC vents

Coastal humidity and air conditioning running most of the year create damp corners in bathrooms, behind wardrobes and around AC outlets. We clean the visible surface mould from tiles, grout and silicone and wipe the vent grilles. If mould keeps coming back in the same place, that usually points to a leak or ventilation problem, and we'll tell you what we saw so your landlord or maintenance team can check it.

Questions about Deep cleaning

How long does a deep clean take?

It depends on the size and condition of the home. When you send the details we tell you how many people we'll send and roughly how long they'll need.

Do you clean inside the oven and fridge?

Yes, on request. Please empty the fridge before the team arrives.

Do I need to empty the cabinets?

For inside-cabinet cleaning, yes. If you can't, tell us and the team will empty and refill them, which adds time.

Can you remove all mould?

We clean visible surface mould from tiles, grout and silicone. Mould inside walls or behind fixtures needs maintenance work, and we'll point it out if we see signs of it.
