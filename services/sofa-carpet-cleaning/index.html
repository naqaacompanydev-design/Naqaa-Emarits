Why upholstery needs more than vacuuming

Sand and fine dust work their way deep into fabric, and humidity helps them stick. Add daily use, children and pets, and a sofa can look fine from a distance but feel and smell tired. Extraction pushes cleaning solution into the fibres and pulls it back out with the dirt, which a vacuum can't do.

What we clean

Fabric sofas and armchairs, sectional corners, dining chairs with fabric seats, traditional majlis seating and back cushions, area rugs, fitted carpets in bedrooms and offices, and mattresses. Leather and delicate fabrics such as silk need different care, so tell us the material when you send photos and we'll confirm whether we can clean it at home.

After the clean

Upholstery is left damp, not wet. Keep the AC or a fan running and avoid sitting on it until it is dry to the touch. The team will tell you what to expect for your fabric.

Questions about Sofa & carpet cleaning

Will every stain come out?

Many stains lift fully, but old stains, ink, dye transfer and bleach marks may only fade. The team will tell you before starting which stains are unlikely to come out.

How long until I can use the sofa?

It depends on the fabric and the room's airflow. Keep the AC or a fan on and wait until it feels dry to the touch.

Do you clean leather sofas?

Leather needs a different product and method. Send a photo and we'll confirm.

Can you do sofas together with a home clean?

Yes. Book both in the same visit and tell us so we send the right equipment.
