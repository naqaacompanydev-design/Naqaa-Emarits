Cleaning that fits around your working day

Choose early-morning, evening or weekend visits so the team never interrupts meetings or customers. For busy offices, a daily visit keeps the pantry and washrooms in order; smaller teams often book two or three visits a week. We agree the schedule and the checklist with you before the first visit.

Clinics, showrooms and retail

Showrooms and shops need spotless glass and floors before opening. Clinics and salons need high-touch surfaces cleaned often. Tell us the type of space and any rules your landlord or mall management sets, and we'll plan the visits around them.

Monthly contracts

For regular visits we offer a monthly contract with a fixed schedule and a fixed monthly price agreed in advance. You can add a deep clean or carpet cleaning when you need it.

Questions about Office cleaning

Do you clean after office hours?

Yes. Evening, early-morning and weekend visits are available. We agree on access and alarm arrangements before the first visit.

Who provides the washroom supplies?

Cleaning materials and equipment are ours. Consumables such as soap, tissue and bin liners are usually supplied by the office, or we can agree to supply them.

Can we start with a trial visit?

Yes. Many offices start with a single visit or a deep clean before agreeing a monthly schedule.

Do you clean free zone offices and business centres?

Yes, as long as the building management allows service teams. Tell us the building so we can check the access process.
