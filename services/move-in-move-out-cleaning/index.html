Moving out: hand the keys back with confidence

Tenancy contracts usually ask you to return the unit in the condition you received it, and the landlord or agency will inspect it before the deposit is settled. A professional move-out clean deals with the things inspectors look at: grease in the kitchen, limescale in the bathroom, marks on walls near switches, dust in wardrobes and the balcony floor.

Book the clean after your furniture has left and before the inspection. An empty unit is faster and more thorough to clean.

Moving in: start in a clean home

Even a new or freshly handed-over unit has construction dust, fingerprints and whatever the previous tenant missed. Cleaning before your furniture arrives means every cabinet is clean before you fill it.

Check your building's rules first

Many towers and communities need a move permit or an access pass for service teams, and some only allow work at set hours. Check with your building management before you book and tell us the time window, so the team isn't stopped at the gate.

TipTake photos of every room after the clean. They help if there's any question at inspection.

Questions about Move-in / move-out cleaning

Does the unit need to be empty?

It's best if it is. If some furniture stays, tell us which rooms are affected so we plan the time correctly.

Can you clean on the same day as the movers?

Yes, if the movers finish first. Tell us their expected finish time and we'll schedule the team after it.

Do you clean walls?

We wipe marks around switches, doors and skirting. Repainting or filling holes is maintenance work and not part of cleaning.

Do you need water and electricity in the unit?

Yes. Please make sure both are connected on the day of the clean.
