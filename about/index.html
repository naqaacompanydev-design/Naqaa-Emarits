Where we come from

Naqaa was built in Riyadh, cleaning apartments, villas, majlis seating, mosques and water tanks. Over time we turned what worked into a method: a checklist for each service, the right products for each surface, and a final walkthrough with the client.

How we work in the UAE

The UAE team follows the same method. Every booking starts on WhatsApp with the details of your home, and you get a clear price in AED before anything is booked. The team arrives with its own equipment and materials, works through the checklist for your service, and walks through the result with you before leaving.

What we don't do

We don't add charges after the visit for work you didn't agree to, and we don't do maintenance work such as plumbing, painting or AC repairs. If we notice a problem while cleaning, such as a leak or damaged silicone, we tell you so the right person can fix it.
